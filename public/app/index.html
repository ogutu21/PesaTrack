PesaTrack

Your M-Pesa messages, added for you.

The Android app reads each M-Pesa message as it arrives and adds it to your budget. Nothing to type, even when you're offline.

Download PesaTrack 0.14.0

Android 7 or newer. Free. If the download doesn't start, open it in Google Drive.

Install it in five steps

  1. Open the downloaded file. Tap Open in your browser, or find PesaTrack-0.14.0.apk in your Downloads.
  2. Allow the install. Android asks once whether your browser or Files app may install apps. Switch it on.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, choose Install anyway. The app isn't on the Play Store.
  4. Sign in with your PesaTrack account, or create one.
  5. Allow SMS and notifications when asked. Then open Settings in the app and check the list under Automatic M-Pesa capture. Anything marked ! has a button to fix it.
SMS access is blocked or greyed out

Android can restrict apps installed from a download. Go to Settings, Apps, PesaTrack, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Allow restricted settings. Then open Permissions and allow SMS.

Using an iPhone or another kind of phone?

Open pesatrack-92632.web.app in your browser and choose Add to Home screen. You can add transactions by hand or import your M-Pesa statement. Automatic reading needs the Android app.

Your data stays in your own PesaTrack account. Version 0.14.0.
